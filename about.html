About us

Helping deal teams know which deals hold up

DealBasis provides Financial Diligence and Deal Intelligence for private equity, testing a company’s numbers before the letter of intent so buyers spend their time and fees on the deals worth doing.

Our story

Why we started DealBasis

Most deals that fall apart after a signed letter of intent do so because of what diligence finds. In one study of 75 broken lower-middle-market deals, nearly half died on diligence findings, often after months of exclusivity.1

By the time a buyer learns the numbers don’t hold up, the fees are spent and the time is gone. We started DealBasis to move that first, hard look at the numbers to the very start of a deal.

DealBasis reads what the seller sends, tests every figure against the others, and shows what doesn’t hold up, with the source of every number one click away. Deal teams get a clear answer on whether to move forward, and a committee memo and deck built from the evidence.

Our values

What we stand for

The principles behind every part of the platform.

Every number has a source

A figure you can’t trace isn’t evidence. Every number links to the page, row or cell it came from.

Same documents, same answer

Fixed, tested rules instead of AI models, so results are repeatable and every finding shows its rule.

Decide early

The best time to find a problem is before the letter of intent, when walking away costs nothing.

Your deals stay yours

Each firm works in its own private workspace, with two-factor sign-in and an activity log.
