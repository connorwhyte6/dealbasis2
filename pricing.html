Pricing questions

Who counts as an editor?

Anyone who can upload, edit or approve: workspace Admins and Members, whatever deal role (analyst, manager or partner) they hold. Viewers, such as investment committee members and investors, read deals, memos and decks but can’t change anything, and they’re always free.

Can one person run a whole deal on one seat?

Yes. Deal roles build on each other: a Partner can do everything a Manager or Analyst can, so a solo searcher can take a deal from upload to committee decision alone. More seats are only needed when different people each sign off under their own name.

What happens when the trial ends?

Nothing is deleted. Your workspace becomes read-only: everyone can still open and export every deal, file and report, but nobody can upload or edit. Choose a plan on the admin page and editing comes back as soon as the invoice is paid. An admin can extend the trial once by 7 days. A workspace left without a plan for 30 days after its trial ends may be deleted, so export anything you want to keep.

Can we add seats during the year?

Yes. Request them on the admin page, and added seats are prorated to your renewal date. Moving past 3 or 10 seats lowers the price of every seat at your next renewal.

Do you offer pilots for larger firms?

Yes. Firms that need a signed agreement and a data processing addendum before uploading documents can run a 60-day pilot. Request a demo and we’ll set it up.

How do we pay?

By invoice, yearly in advance. Plans renew each year unless canceled before the renewal date.
