Pricing questions

Who counts as an editor?

Anyone who can upload, edit or approve: workspace Admins and Members, whatever deal role (analyst, manager or partner) they hold. Viewers, such as investment committee members and investors, read deals, memos and decks but can’t change anything, and they’re always free.

Can one person run a whole deal on one seat?

Yes. Deal roles build on each other: a Partner can do everything a Manager or Analyst can, so a solo searcher can take a deal from upload to committee decision alone. More seats are only needed when different people each sign off under their own name.

What happens when the trial ends?

The trial always runs the full 14 days; it can’t be extended or ended early. Buy a plan before it ends and everything carries over: deals, files and the team. If it ends without a plan, its deals, documents and files are locked: kept for 7 days but not openable or exportable. Buy a plan in that week and everything comes back exactly as it was. After the 7 days they are deleted and can’t be recovered; your account stays, and you can still buy a plan, starting with an empty workspace. Each email gets one free trial.

Can we add seats during the year?

Yes. Add them under Manage billing on the admin page, and added seats are prorated to your renewal date. Moving past 3 or 10 seats lowers the price of every seat at your next renewal.Yes. Do you offer pilots for larger firms?

Yes: the design partner pilot above, for three firms. It ends after 60 days with nothing more charged; you choose whether to subscribe, at half the list price for the first year if you do within 30 days. After the pilot the workspace is read-only until you subscribe, and if you don’t, its deals and files are deleted 30 days after the pilot ends. Firms that need a signed agreement and a data processing addendum before uploading documents can request a demo, and we’ll set the pilot up under one.

How do we pay?

By card or US bank account through Stripe, yearly in advance. Plans renew each year unless canceled before the renewal date, and you can change seats, update payment details, download invoices or cancel any time under Manage billing.By invoice, yearly in advance, payable by ACH, wire or card. Plans renew each year unless canceled before the renewal date. Request an invoice for your seats on your admin page.

Can we cancel, and are fees refunded?

You can cancel any time under Manage billing on the admin page. The plan stays active until the end of the period you’ve paid for and doesn’t renew. Fees, including the design partner pilot fee, are non-refundable. When a plan ends, the workspace becomes read-only for 30 days: everyone can still open and export every deal, file and report, and buying a plan again brings everything back. After 30 days, its deals, documents and files are deleted automatically.
