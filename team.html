Team

Meet the founders

DealBasis is built and led by its two co-founders, who bring together engineering, finance and Sales.

Connor Whyte

Co-Founder

Lead Engineer and Go-to-Market Strategist

Connor is the engineer behind DealBasis. He designs and builds the platform end to end, from how documents are read and checked to the committee memo and deck, and also leads Go-to-Market, drawing on hands-on B2B software Sales experience. He holds a BS in Business Administration from the University of Arizona and is an MS in Finance Candidate at Arizona State University.

Queenie Valencia

Co-Founder

Head of Sales and Client Relationships

Queenie leads Sales and the relationships with the deal teams DealBasis serves. She brings experience in Venture Capital, giving the company an investor's view of how buyers judge a deal and what earns their trust.
