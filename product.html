Features

Two workspaces: Financial Diligence and Deal Intelligence

Financial Diligence tests the numbers. Deal Intelligence turns them into a price, a risk score and a committee decision. Both work from the same record, with the source of every number one click away.

Financial Diligence

Are the numbers real?

Quality of earnings

Every add-back in management's bridge tested and accepted or rejected with a reason, ending in an EBITDA you can defend.

Working capital

Four definitions side by side, with the trailing twelve-month peg and the closing adjustment for the one in your deal.

Debt tie-outs

Balances tied to the balance sheet and lender confirmations, plus debt mentioned in the documents but never recorded.

Cash flow and cross-checks

How much of EBITDA turns into cash, and every figure in the text checked against the statements and ledger.

Deal Intelligence

What is it worth, and should we do it?

Deal economics

Enterprise value and equity value at your multiple, with the sensitivity and the returns case, all on diligence-adjusted EBITDA.

Risk scoring

Over 30 tests run against the record, each finding with its source, rolled into a pre-LOI confidence score.

Issues and seller questions

Every finding becomes an issue with an owner, and the questions to put to the seller are drafted for you.

Committee readout

The memo and a committee deck drafted from the evidence, ready to present or download as PowerPoint, Word, PDF or Excel.

Who it's for

Built for the people who screen deals
