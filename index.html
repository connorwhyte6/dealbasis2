How it works

From documents to committee in three steps

No templates to fill in and no figures to retype.

1

Upload whatever you have

A single CIM is enough to start. Add financial statements, ledgers, balance sheets, lender confirmations, management responses and SEC filings, as PDF, Excel, Word or CSV. Scanned pages work too.

2

DealBasis checks everything

Figures are read from tables and text, tested against each other and the ledger, and every citation is re-read before a finding is shown.

3

Review and present

Click any number to see where it came from, adjust your assumptions, and take the generated memo and deck to committee.
