FAQ

Questions deal teams ask

What do I need to upload?

CIMs and management presentations, financial statements, general ledgers, monthly balance sheets, lender confirmations, management's answers to diligence questions and SEC filings. PDF, Excel, Word, PowerPoint, CSV, text and email files all work, and so does a zipped folder of them; scanned pages are read with text recognition. You don't need a full data room: one CIM or a seller's notes is enough to start, and each document you add is checked against the others.

Does AI read my documents?

No. DealBasis uses fixed, tested rules, so the same documents always produce the same result, and every finding shows the rule behind it.

What happens when a figure only appears in the text?

DealBasis reads figures written in sentences, like "revenue of $3.3 million in 2024", and compares them with the financial statements for the same line and year, allowing for rounding. A match is confirmed; a difference is reported and the statement figure is used. If no statement covers it, the figure from the text is used and labeled as such.

How do I know a finding is right?

Every figure links to the page and line, spreadsheet cell or ledger row it came from. Before a finding is shown, each of its citations is re-read against the document to confirm it shows the figure.

Where are my files stored?

Documents are read in your browser when you upload them. The results and the original files are stored in your firm's own workspace, and access rules are enforced by the database.

Can my team work on the same deal?

Yes. Invite colleagues, set who can edit or only view, assign issues and leave comments on findings.

Do I need an account to try it?

No. The Project Saguaro sample deal opens without an account, including the memo and deck downloads. Create an account when you want to upload your own deals.
